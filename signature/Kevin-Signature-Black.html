Copy puts the signature on your clipboard as rich HTML. In Gmail go Settings → See all settings → Signature, click into the box and paste with ⌘V — not paste-and-match-style, that strips the black.

One thing to know about black: a few clients (Outlook on Windows, some dark-mode setups) can shift background colors. These are built with bgcolor attributes as well as CSS, which is the most reliable way to hold a fill — but if you want the version that is physically impossible to break, BC is the safest of the three, and Option 03 is the white-background original.
